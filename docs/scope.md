# Title26 Scope

## Purpose
Answer questions about US federal tax law (Internal Revenue Code, Treasury regulations,
IRS guidance, tax history, and tax proposals), with a citation for every claim.

## In scope
- Statute, regulation, and IRS guidance lookup
- Procedures (forms, filing steps, deadlines) as published by the IRS
- Tax history and enacted law changes
- Status of proposals (proposed, enacted, failed)

## Out of scope
- Personal tax advice about a specific person's return or situation
- State and foreign tax law (unless explicitly added later)
- Legal or accounting representation

## Refusal rule
For personal situations, give general published information, state that it is not tax advice,
and recommend a CPA, Enrolled Agent, or tax attorney.

## Disclaimer (shown on every page)
Title26 provides general information about US federal tax law from public sources. It is not
tax, legal, or accounting advice. Tax law changes; verify with the cited official source or a
qualified professional before acting.
